ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

What Is Medicine and Surgery?

Medicine and Surgery is a healthcare discipline concerned with understanding the human body, identifying diseases, preventing illness and managing health conditions.

Students develop scientific knowledge and clinical skills through classroom learning, laboratory studies and supervised clinical training.

What You Will Learn

🧠

Medical Sciences

Understand anatomy, physiology, biochemistry and the normal functioning of the human body.

🔬

Diagnosis

Learn how medical history, examination and investigations help identify diseases.

🏥

Clinical Practice

Develop clinical knowledge through supervised patient-care experiences.

❤️

Patient Management

Understand principles of treatment, disease prevention and patient support.

AREAS OF STUDY

Major Subjects in Medicine and Surgery

Explore important medical disciplines commonly included in medical education. Course structures vary between universities.

Anatomy

Study the structure and organisation of the human body.

Physiology

Understand how organs and body systems function.

Biochemistry

Explore chemical processes and reactions in living organisms.

Pathology

Study diseases, their causes and effects on body tissues.

Pharmacology

Understand medicines and how they act in the body.

Microbiology

Study microorganisms and their relationship with disease.

Internal Medicine

Explore the diagnosis and management of adult medical conditions.

Surgery

Study surgical principles and the management of conditions requiring operations.

Paediatrics

Study the health and medical care of infants and children.

Obstetrics and Gynaecology

Explore reproductive health, pregnancy and childbirth.

Psychiatry

Understand mental health conditions and their clinical management.

Community Medicine

Study public health, disease prevention and community health.

CLINICAL LEARNING

From Classroom to Clinical Practice

Medical education connects scientific knowledge with supervised clinical experiences. Students gradually develop the ability to assess patients, understand clinical findings and contribute to patient management.

  • Medical history taking
  • Physical examination principles
  • Clinical reasoning and decision-making
  • Laboratory and diagnostic investigations
  • Patient communication
  • Professional ethics and patient safety

Skills Developed During Training

🔎

Clinical Reasoning

Develop the ability to analyse clinical information and consider possible diagnoses.

🗣️

Communication

Communicate clearly and respectfully with patients and healthcare teams.

📚

Scientific Thinking

Apply scientific knowledge when understanding health and disease.

🤝

Teamwork

Work collaboratively with other healthcare professionals.

FUTURE PATHWAYS

Career Opportunities

Medical graduates may pursue different career pathways following the required qualifications, internship, registration and further specialist training.

Medical Doctor

Provide medical assessment and care to patients.

Medical Specialist

Develop advanced expertise in a specialised medical field.

Medical Researcher

Contribute to research that advances medical knowledge.

Public Health Professional

Contribute to disease prevention and population health programmes.
