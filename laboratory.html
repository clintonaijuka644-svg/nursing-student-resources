What Is Medical Laboratory Science?

Medical Laboratory Science is a health profession concerned with analysing blood, body fluids and tissue samples to help detect, diagnose and monitor diseases. It plays an important role in modern healthcare.

Practical Laboratory Learning

Practical training helps students develop the skills needed to handle specimens, operate laboratory equipment, perform investigations and interpret laboratory results.

  • Specimen collection and handling
  • Microscopy and laboratory techniques
  • Use of laboratory equipment
  • Quality assurance and safety
