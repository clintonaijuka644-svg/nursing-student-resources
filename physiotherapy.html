What Is Physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession that helps people develop, maintain and restore movement and physical function. It uses assessment, therapeutic exercises and other rehabilitation approaches to support recovery and wellbeing.

Practical Rehabilitation Training

Practical training helps students develop the knowledge and skills needed to assess patients, plan rehabilitation and support safe recovery.

  • Movement and mobility assessment
  • Therapeutic exercise techniques
  • Posture and gait assessment
  • Patient communication and education
