What is the website about

This platform is designed to help students who are planning to join or have recently joined health and medical-related programmes. Whether you are studying nursing, medicine, pharmacy, medical-related laboratory science, physiotherapy or another health science course. You can find yourself information, study materials, and guidance here.

Our goal is to make the transition into university easier by helping students understand what to expect, what to study and where to find useful learning resources.
