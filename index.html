What Is This Website About?

This platform is designed to support students who are planning to join or have recently joined health and medical-related programmes. Whether you are studying Nursing, Medicine, Pharmacy, Medical Laboratory Science, Physiotherapy or another health science programme, you can find useful information, study materials and academic guidance here.

Our goal is to make the transition into university easier by helping students understand what to expect, what to study and where to find reliable learning resources.
