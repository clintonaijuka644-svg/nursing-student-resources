What is Bachelor of Nursing science?

Bachelor of Nursing Science(BSN) is a university degree that prepares students to become professional nurses. It combines classroom learning, practical skills and clinical experience to help students understand the human body, care for patients, prevent illness and promote health.

During the programme, students study subjects such as Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pharmacology and different areas of Nursing. They also develop communication, clinical and critical-thinking skills.
